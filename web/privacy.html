Flash Light Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Applies to Flash Light Pro for Android and iOS (Android package com.claudysoft.flashlightpro)

Flash Light Pro is published by ClaudySoft (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and with whom it is shared.

Flash Light Pro works on your device. The flashlight, SOS, strobe, Morse, and screen light need no account and no network. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob after the consent required in your region, and an optional one-time purchase removes them.

Data stored on your device

Preferences such as language, default mode, torch brightness, screen brightness, timer preset, shake sensitivity, haptic feedback, keep-screen-awake, and reduced-animation settings are stored locally by the operating system. They are never transmitted by the app.

The app also stores ad-frequency limits, your ad consent choice, and Remove ads purchase status locally so ads stay within limits and an owned entitlement is recognized immediately at startup.

On Android, the Quick Settings tile and home screen widget also store the last torch state locally so they can show the correct state after a process restart.

Hardware access

The app uses:

  • the camera flash (torch) through the platform torch API;
  • screen brightness while Screen Light is active;
  • haptics for optional feedback;
  • the accelerometer only while Shake to toggle is enabled: in the foreground, and on Android through a specialUse foreground service with a persistent notification when the app is in the background.

The app does not capture camera images, microphone audio, location, contacts, photos, or files. It does not request camera permission, microphone permission, or location permission. On Android 13 and later it asks for notification permission so the background flashlight and shake services can show their controls; denying it does not stop the services.

Advertising

The free version may show banner, native, interstitial, and app-open ads from Google AdMob on informational screens and in Settings. Ads are requested only after the consent required in your region, and never while a light is active, while an update is required, or during a permission or initialization flow.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from the device, app interaction and advertising data, and diagnostics under Google's own privacy terms. That information is shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in those terms. The developer does not receive or store your personal data from AdMob. You can review or change your consent choices at any time with Privacy options in Settings.

Remove ads purchase

Remove ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app never receives your payment details. Purchase information is processed by the store under its own privacy policy. The entitlement is cached on your device and reconciled with the store; you can restore it at any time from Settings. The purchase removes all ads and is not a subscription.

No analytics or crash uploads

The app includes no analytics SDK and no remote crash reporting. If the app closes unexpectedly, no report is uploaded. The flashlight, SOS, strobe, Morse, and screen light keep working without a connection, even when ads cannot load, consent cannot be obtained, or the store is unavailable.

Retention and deletion

Settings, ad-frequency limits, consent choices, and entitlement status stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. You can delete this local data at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Flash Light Pro → Storage → Clear data.
  • iOS: delete the app; this removes its local data.

Reinstalling lets the store restore an owned Remove ads purchase. You can change ad consent at any time with Privacy options in Settings. For data held by Google as an advertising provider, use the controls described in Google's privacy terms linked above.

System integrations

  • Android Quick Settings tile and home screen widget: toggle the torch and show state without opening the app.
  • iOS Shortcuts: on, off, and toggle actions run through the system intent while the system allows app intents to execute.
  • iOS does not provide Android-style Quick Settings tiles or home screen widgets for unrestricted background flashlight control.
  • Volume buttons are not intercepted because that would require accessibility or root access, which this app does not request.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). It has no accounts, no user-generated content, and no social features, and the developer collects no personal data. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are requested only after the consent required in your region.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new text will ship with the app and on this page.
