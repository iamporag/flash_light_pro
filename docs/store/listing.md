TORCH AND SCREEN LIGHT

SIGNALS AND TOOLS

• Adjustable strobe with a photosensitivity warning
• Morse code playback for A-Z and 0-9
• Auto-off timer and keep-screen-awake option

SYSTEM INTEGRATIONS
• Android: Quick Settings tile and home screen widget toggle the torch directly
• iOS: Shortcuts actions for turning the flashlight on, off, or toggling it

MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE
• English and Bengali interface with a manual language switch

• Remembers your mode, brightness, timer, and language choices

• No account, analytics, or tracking SDK
• Ads appear only on informational screens, never on the flashlight screen
• One-time Remove ads purchase clears every ad
• Preferences stay on your device

Notes: iOS does not provide Android-style Quick Settings tiles or home screen
widgets for unrestricted background flashlight control. Volume buttons are not
intercepted because that would require accessibility or root access.
